## 3. The candidate: `t13-keep-one-k2` and its exact difference from K1

`src/miaosuan_agent/experiments/t13_keep_one_k2.py` is a copy of Sprint 30's frozen K1 module (which stays unchanged
under its own identity) generated by declared substitutions. Unchanged from K1: the held-objective definition, the
centre-hex occupants, the departing and remaining classification, the trigger, eligibility levels A (speed), B (route),
D (embark and disembark timers) and E (exactly one departing `baseline-v2` MOVE), the selection by the longest
free-flow travel time with the lower unit id on ties, one holder per objective, the single edit (that MOVE withheld,
nothing added, replaced, reordered or constructed) and statelessness.

**The one change, level C** (renamed `C_no_other_transition`):

| Unit state | K1 | K2 |
|---|---|---|
| no stop transition (`stop` not 0 and `move_to_stop_remain_time` not positive) | passes unless `change_state_remain_time` is positive; a missing field counts as no transition | identical |
| a stop transition in the documented post-arrival form: every one of `speed`, `stop`, `move_to_stop_remain_time`, `flag_force_stop`, `change_state_remain_time`, `get_on_remain_time`, `get_off_remain_time` present as a number; `speed` 0; `stop` 0; `0 < move_to_stop_remain_time <= 75`; `flag_force_stop` 0; the three other timers 0 | fails | **passes** |
| any other form of a stop transition (a missing or non-numeric field, a timer of 0 or above 75 with `stop` 0, `stop` 1 with a positive timer, a deferred stop order, a moving speed, a state or transport timer not 0) | fails | fails (ambiguous: fail closed) |

The semantics on which the admitted state rests: the documented rule that a stopping unit serves a 75 s move-to-stop
transition; the recorded facts that at the natural end of a path `stop` turns 1 exactly 75 steps after the path empties
(`docs/T7_DESIGN.md`, B-2, 141 of 141 arrivals), that a stationary unit has `stop` 0 exactly when its timer is positive
(`docs/SPRINT28_T12_O1.md`), that a deferred stop order shows as `flag_force_stop` 1 with the timer at 0
(`docs/PS1_ENGINE_PROBE.md`) and that a stop order is listed only for units with a move path (`docs/T7_DESIGN.md`, B-1).
Neither `baseline-v2` nor the candidate ever issues a stop order, so for their units this state follows the end of a
move. Withholding the MOVE of such a unit needs no new action and cancels no accepted path; route, speed and transport
conditions still exclude. A syntax-tree test shows that level C is the only code difference from K1 (with the identity
constants and the four new names that implement it), and a digest test that K1 is byte-identical to its Sprint 30 pin.

**Mechanism.** A withholding is *retained* when, at the candidate's next decision, the holder is still an own operator
on the same centre with an empty move path. A garrison episode runs from a holder's first withholding until it is
absent (destroyed), its MOVE is emitted (released; the reason is the objective's state then: not held, another
occupant remains, the holder no longer eligible, or another holder selected), it leaves the centre without a released
MOVE, or the game ends; it is *executed* when at least one withholding is retained. Recorded per episode: whether the
holder was settling at the start, whether it completed its transition on the centre, decisions on the centre,
withholdings (each one a repeated `baseline-v2` MOVE), duration, end and release reason, the objective lost while the
holder stood on it, and the onward facts of its withheld MOVE's destination (first owned, and when). **Mechanism
failures** (any one: `K2_PILOT_REJECT`): **M1** a holder found off the centre, or with a move path, at the decision after
a withholding although no MOVE was emitted for it; **M2** a game without an executed episode (the preflight verified an
opportunity in all four configurations); **M3** own ground units waiting (a move path, speed not positive) with a
holder's centre as their next hex while that centre holds four own ground units, for a run of at least 300 consecutive
decisions (the registered hold limit of Sprint 25's garrison rule; shorter runs are reported).
